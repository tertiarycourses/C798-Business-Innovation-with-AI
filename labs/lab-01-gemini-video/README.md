# Lab 1 — Create a Business Story Video with Gemini

## Alignment

LO1 · Topic 1

## Goal

Compare a generative-AI content workflow with a traditional production workflow.

Create a short business-innovation video with Gemini/Veo, then compare the human decisions, cycle time and controls with a conventional production process.

## Deliverable

A 6–8 second business story video, prompt record and before/after workflow comparison

## Tools

Gemini, Veo video generation, browser download

## Configuration and constraints

- Output: 16:9 video lasting approximately 6-8 seconds.
- Review people, text, logos, claims, brand fit and unintended personal data before approval.
- Record the prompt, model/service, date, reviewer and approval decision.

## Copy-paste brief

```text
Create a 16:9, 6-8 second cinematic video for a Singapore SME. Show an operations coordinator reviewing an AI-assisted service dashboard, then approving a customer update. Use natural daylight, realistic people and calm camera movement. Do not show logos, readable personal data, invented performance claims, distorted hands or unreadable text.
```

## Procedure

1. Open Gemini, choose video generation and confirm your account has Veo access.
2. Write a shot brief covering subject, setting, action, camera, lighting, audio and exclusions.
3. Generate a 16:9 video for a Singapore SME introducing an AI-assisted service workflow.
4. Review accuracy, brand safety and unwanted text; revise one variable at a time.
5. Download the final clip and record the prompt, model, date and human approval decision.
6. Map the old and AI-enabled production workflows, including where human judgement remains.

## Evidence pack

- Business brief and baseline problem statement.
- Prompt or instruction version and approved source list.
- Screenshots, files, diffs or execution records showing the workflow and controls.
- Test results for normal, edge, malicious and tool-failure cases.
- Human review decision, outstanding risks and next recommended gate.

## Acceptance check

The clip plays, matches the brief, contains no invented brand claims, and the comparison explains how generative AI changes cycle time without removing human accountability.

## Safety boundary

Use only non-confidential training data and approved accounts. Do not connect production credentials or approve external or consequential actions during this lab.
